FOOD SAFETY

Freshness is enforced, not assumed

Three built-in safeguards keep every rescued meal safe to serve.

Live expiry countdown

Each listing shows exact minutes left before the safe donation window closes.

Storage declaration

Donors declare temperature and packaging conditions before publishing.

Auto de-listing

Expired items are removed from search and flagged in the expiry alerts console.
