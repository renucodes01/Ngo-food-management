HOW IT WORKS

Four steps from surplus to served

Designed with food-safety windows, verified organisations and live logistics.

🍴
STEP 1
Donor posts surplus

Restaurants, canteens and event halls list leftover food with quantity, freshness window and pickup address.

📍
STEP 2
Nearby NGO requests

Verified NGOs see live listings sorted by distance and expiry, then request the servings they need.

🚚
STEP 3
Pickup & delivery

A volunteer route is generated, live-tracked on the map and confirmed with a delivery proof.

❤️
STEP 4
Meals served

Every completed pickup adds to your impact score, CO₂ saved and the community leaderboard.
