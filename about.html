ABOUT US

We believe no edible meal
should reach a landfill

FoodShare started in a college canteen with 40 unclaimed lunch boxes. Today it is a city-wide surplus food rescue network.

🎯
Our mission

Move surplus food to people in need within its safe window, every single day.

🌿
Our vision

A zero-food-waste city where every kitchen has a rescue route within 3 km.

👥
Our community

320 donors, 150 verified NGOs and hundreds of volunteers coordinating daily pickups.

Privacy policy

We collect only the information needed to coordinate a safe food handover: contact details, pickup addresses and donation records. Personal data is never sold, and NGO documents are used solely for verification. Donors and NGOs may request deletion of their account data at any time.

Terms of use

Donors confirm that listed food is edible, correctly stored and within its safe window. NGOs confirm they will distribute food promptly and log beneficiaries served. FoodShare facilitates the connection and logistics but does not take ownership of the food at any stage.
